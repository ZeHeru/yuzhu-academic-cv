This project developed a surface-enhanced Raman scattering imaging approach for
fingerprint recognition and chemical mapping under varied surface and
environmental conditions.

<!--more-->

The work involved collecting fingerprint samples, performing SERS imaging, and
analyzing chemical maps for non-invasive identification of residues such as
drugs or nicotine.
