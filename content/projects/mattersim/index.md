During an AI for Science internship at Microsoft Research, I contributed
benchmarks and computational workflows supporting materials-model development,
training-data generation, and property-prediction studies.

<!--more-->

The work involved high-throughput materials datasets, inference on models up to
1B parameters, and Azure pipelines supporting up to 10K concurrent tasks.
Tools included VASP, atomate2, joblib, phonopy, phono3py, ESEN, MACE, and ORB.
