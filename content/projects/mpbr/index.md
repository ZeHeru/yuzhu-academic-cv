MPBR explores recognition in open-world settings where unknown fine-grained
categories appear at inference time. The method combines forward refinement of
visual features with backward multimodal consistency constraints.

<!--more-->

My work included developing benchmark pipelines and evaluating leading
open-set-recognition models on iNat2021-OSR. Analysis used t-SNE, Grad-CAM,
Swin Transformer backbones, and HAN-OSFGR.
